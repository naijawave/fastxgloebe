How tracking works

  1. 1Goods receivedYour shipment is booked in, a tracking code is generated automatically and a receipt is issued.
  2. 2We move itSea, air or road — the location is updated at every checkpoint, and pinned on the map.
  3. 3You track itEnter your FXG tracking code and see the current location, timeline and map.
  4. 4DeliveredYour goods arrive at their destination, confirmed on the timeline.

A leading Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier (NVOCC) operator

fastxgloebe operates as a leading Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier (NVOCC) operator in the world. Our NVOCC business encompasses cargo consolidation and door to door / port to port sea freight forwarding services.

From less-than-container-load (LCL) consolidation to full-container (FCL) movements, our team plans the route, handles the documentation and keeps your shipment visible from the day it is booked until the day it is delivered. One tracking code, one timeline, one map — and a support team you can reach at any point of the journey.

About Us

fastxgloebe is a freight forwarding and haulage company offering shipments worldwide. From our base in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, we move cargo for individuals and businesses — vehicles, household goods, commercial consignments and consolidated loads — by sea, air and road.

Our dedicated staff equips you with everything you need to move your cargo — delivering your shipments anywhere in the world, with honest updates at every checkpoint: a tracking code, a timeline and a map pin placed by our own team, so you always know where your goods are.

Our Mission

To provide shipping and logistics services which transcend the needs and expectations of our customers — safe, cost-effective and transparent from booking to delivery.

Our Goals

  • Transparency first — every shipment tracked, every checkpoint visible to the customer in real time.
  • Safe handling — goods received, packed, unitized and moved as if they were our own, arriving without damage.
  • Cost-effective routes — the right sailing, flight or road route for each budget, without cutting corners on care.
  • Communication that never sleeps — a support team and a customer chat that answer, update and reassure at every stage.
  • Growth with our clients — long-term relationships built on deliveries that arrive as promised, year after year.
